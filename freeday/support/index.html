Common questions

How do I connect accounts from Apple Wallet?

When Freeday offers to connect Apple Wallet, iOS shows a permission prompt. Allow access, then choose the accounts you want to share and how far back to share from. Only eligible Wallet accounts appear, and connecting is always optional.

How do I stop sharing Apple Wallet data?

Open the iOS Settings app, go to Privacy & Security, find the Wallet/financial data section and turn off access for Freeday. Freeday then stops receiving new data from Wallet.

How do I delete my data?

Your records live on your iPhone and, if enabled, your own iCloud. Delete them in the app [TO CONFIRM: where the in-app delete option is, e.g. Settings > Delete all data], or delete Freeday from your iPhone. To remove synced copies, manage Freeday's data in your iCloud storage settings. Because we don't hold copies of your records, we can't delete them for you — but we're happy to walk you through it.

Does Freeday give financial advice?

No. Freeday helps you organise and understand your own financial information. It does not provide regulated financial advice. For advice on your circumstances, speak to a qualified, regulated adviser.
