Freeday Privacy Policy

Effective: 19 October 2026 · Applies to the Freeday app for iPhone

The short version
  • Your financial records are stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account.
  • Freeday reads Apple Wallet data only after you grant permission, and only for the accounts and dates you choose.
  • The optional goal assistant sends your question and related transactions to our own server in the UK. Apple Wallet data is never sent. Section 5 explains the details.
  • No advertising, no data sales, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • You can revoke access and delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Freeday is made by JCW Solutions, operated by Joshua Williams, based in the UK ("we", "us").

For any personal data we process about you in connection with Freeday, we are the data controller under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can contact us about privacy at josh@jcw.solutions.

3. Apple Wallet data (FinanceKit)

If you choose, Freeday can use Apple's FinanceKit framework to read financial information from eligible accounts in Apple Wallet on your iPhone.

  • Permission first. iOS asks for your permission before Freeday can access any Wallet data. Freeday cannot access it otherwise.
  • You control the scope. You choose which accounts to share and the earliest date to share from.
  • What Freeday reads: account details (such as the account name and type), balances, and transactions (such as date, amount, merchant name and description) for the accounts you share.
  • Why: only to provide Freeday's financial management features to you — showing your transactions, balances, net worth, spending trends and progress towards your goals.
  • Where it goes: Wallet data is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account as described in section 4. Wallet data is never sent off your device: not to our server and not to any service provider. It is excluded from the goal assistant described in section 5.
  • Never used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions or profiling by third parties, and never sold, rented or shared with data brokers.
  • Revoking access. You can stop sharing at any time in the iOS Settings app (under Privacy & Security). Freeday then stops receiving new Wallet data, and you can delete what it already holds (section 12).

4. Where your data is stored

On your iPhone. Your financial records are stored in Freeday's storage on your device and protected by iOS data protection.

In your iCloud (optional). If iCloud is enabled for Freeday, your records sync between your devices through your own private iCloud account. This data is held by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's own privacy policy; JCW Solutions cannot access your private iCloud data. You can turn off iCloud sync for Freeday in iOS Settings.

Apart from the processing described in section 5 and support messages you send us, we do not hold copies of your financial records on our own servers.

5. Features that process data off your device

Freeday's goal assistant lets you ask questions about your financial goals. To answer, the app sends your question and the related transactions to an AI model that we run ourselves, on our own server in the UK, using the open-source Ollama software. No third-party AI company receives your data. The goal assistant is optional.

7. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal or financial data.
  • We do not show advertising in Freeday or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.
  • We do not use your financial data to make credit or lending decisions, or share it with anyone who does.
  • We do not use information from our consultancy clients in Freeday, or Freeday data in our consultancy work.

8. Who we share information with

We share information only with:

  • Apple, when you use iCloud sync or download Freeday from the App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf under contract and only on our instructions: We do not use a third-party AI provider; the goal assistant runs on our own server (section 5). Support emails are handled by our email hosting provider.
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to.
  • A successor, if Freeday is ever transferred to another organisation, which would have to protect your data as this policy describes. We would tell you first.

9. International transfers

Freeday data that we process ourselves (section 5) stays in the UK. Data in your iCloud account is handled by Apple under its own terms.

10. How long we keep information

  • Data on your device and in your iCloud: until you delete it or delete the app. We cannot delete it for you because we do not hold it.
  • Data processed for features in section 5: Kept on our server for up to 24 hours to provide the answer, then deleted.
  • Support emails: up to 12 months after your enquiry is resolved

11. Security

Freeday relies on iOS security features, including data protection on your device and encrypted transfer to iCloud. Data sent to our server for the goal assistant: sent over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your data occurs, we will act and notify you and the Information Commissioner's Office where the law requires.

12. Your rights and choices

Controls you have in the app and in iOS:

  • Revoke Apple Wallet access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Delete your records within Freeday (Settings, then Delete all data), or delete the app to remove its on-device data.
  • Turn off iCloud sync for Freeday in iOS Settings, and manage stored iCloud data in your Apple Account settings.

Your UK GDPR rights. You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to our processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To use these rights, email josh@jcw.solutions. We will respond within one month. Because most Freeday data stays on your device and in your iCloud, many requests are best handled directly in the app.

13. Children

Freeday is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change how Freeday handles your data, we will update this policy and its effective date. For significant changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
