3. Apple Wallet data (FinanceKit)

If you choose, Freeday can use Apple's FinanceKit framework to read financial information from eligible accounts in Apple Wallet on your iPhone.

  • Permission first. iOS asks for your permission before Freeday can access any Wallet data. Freeday cannot access it otherwise.
  • You control the scope. You choose which accounts to share and the earliest date to share from.
  • What Freeday reads: account details (such as the account name and type), balances, and transactions (such as date, amount, merchant name and description) for the accounts you share.
  • Why: only to provide Freeday's financial management features to you — showing your transactions, balances, net worth, spending trends and progress towards your goals.
  • Where it goes: Wallet data is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account as described in section 4. [TO CONFIRM: say whether any Wallet data ever leaves the device for the AI feature in section 5 — if not, state 'Wallet data is not sent to our servers or to any service provider.']
  • Never used for advertising, marketing, credit decisions or profiling by third parties, and never sold, rented or shared with data brokers.
  • Revoking access. You can stop sharing at any time in the iOS Settings app (under Privacy & Security). Freeday then stops receiving new Wallet data, and you can delete what it already holds (section 12).

4. Where your data is stored

On your iPhone. Your financial records are stored in Freeday's storage on your device and protected by iOS data protection.

In your iCloud (optional). If iCloud is enabled for Freeday, your records sync between your devices through your own private iCloud account. This data is held by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's own privacy policy; JCW Solutions cannot access your private iCloud data. You can turn off iCloud sync for Freeday in iOS Settings.

Apart from the processing described in section 5 and support messages you send us, we do not hold copies of your financial records on our own servers.
