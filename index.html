Consultancy

Where we help

Grounded in hands-on experience as a Business Technical Analyst — working between business stakeholders, technology teams and delivery.

Business process analysis

Map how work really happens today, find the friction, and agree what needs to change before anything is built.

Technical requirements

Turn goals and constraints into clear, testable requirements that business and technical teams both understand.

Solution readiness

Check that a solution, its dependencies and the people using it are genuinely ready before go-live.

Implementation support

Keep delivery moving: clarify scope questions, track decisions and resolve issues as they surface.

Release planning & test environments

Stakeholder coordination

How we work

Deliberate, discreet, practical

  • Confidential by default. Client information stays with the client engagement. It is never used in our own products or shared with other clients.
  • Scoped to what's needed. We agree outcomes and deliverables up front and only take on work we can support well.
  • Clear outputs. Requirements, process maps, readiness checklists and release plans your team can keep using after we leave.
