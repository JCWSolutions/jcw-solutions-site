Common questions

How do I connect accounts from Apple Wallet?

Go to More → Settings → Bank Connections. When Freeday offers to connect Apple Wallet, iOS shows a permission prompt. Allow access, then choose the accounts you want to share. Only eligible Wallet accounts appear, and connecting is always optional. Apple Wallet connections need Apple's approval for Freeday, so they may show as coming soon.

Dawn doesn't see your Wallet spending unless you turn on Let Dawn see Wallet spending on the same screen.

How do I bring my bank data in?

Connect Apple Wallet (UK and US), or go to More → Settings → Bank Connections to import your bank's CSV export or link a Google Sheet your bank keeps up to date.

Where's my data?

On your devices, and in your own private iCloud if you're signed in. We can't see it, so we can't recover it for you. Keep iCloud on so you can restore it on a new phone.

Can I use Freeday on my iPad, Mac or Apple Watch?

Freeday syncs through your own iCloud, so every device signed in to the same Apple Account keeps a full copy, and your Notion or Trading 212 keys follow you through iCloud Keychain. To sync straight away, use Sync with iCloud now on the Account screen (tap your name at the top of More). Your Apple Watch shows headline figures and bills due soon from your iPhone.

How do I share with my partner?

Go to More → Household and invite them. You each choose which totals to share, such as balances or monthly saving; transactions are never shared. Either of you can stop sharing there at any time.

What has Dawn changed?

Everything Dawn has changed at your request is listed in Settings → Data → Changes made with Dawn.

Can I lock Freeday?

Yes. In Settings → Privacy you can lock the app, or just Wealth, with Face ID or Touch ID, and hide amounts in widgets.

How do I stop sharing Apple Wallet data?

Open the iOS Settings app, go to Privacy & Security, find the Wallet/financial data section and turn off access for Freeday. Freeday then stops receiving new data from Wallet.

How do I delete my data?

Your records live on your iPhone and, if enabled, your own iCloud. Delete them in the app with Settings → Data → Delete all my data, or delete Freeday from your devices. This doesn't change anything at your bank, in Apple Wallet or in Notion. To remove synced copies, manage Freeday's data in your iCloud storage settings. Because we don't hold copies of your records, we can't delete them for you — but we're happy to walk you through it.

Does Freeday give financial advice?

No. Freeday helps you organise and understand your own financial information. It does not provide regulated financial advice. For advice on your circumstances, speak to a qualified, regulated adviser.

Where can I read how my data is used?

See the Freeday Privacy Policy.
