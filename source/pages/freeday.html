Apple Wallet

Connect accounts from Apple Wallet — only if you choose to

Freeday can use Apple's FinanceKit to read balances and transactions from eligible accounts in Apple Wallet. Nothing is shared unless you give permission:

  • iOS asks for your permission before Freeday can see any Wallet data.
  • You choose which accounts to share.
  • You can stop sharing at any time in iOS Settings, and Freeday stops receiving new data.
  • Wallet data is processed on your iPhone and is never sent to us or anyone else as transactions, balances or account details. It is never used for advertising and never sold.
  • Dawn only sees your Wallet spending totals if you switch that on.

Exactly what is read, where it's stored and for how long is set out in the Freeday Privacy Policy.

Privacy by design

Your data stays yours

On your devices and your iCloud

Freeday stores your financial records on your devices and, if you use iCloud, syncs them through your own private iCloud account. We can't open your iCloud data.

Dawn, an AI coach that keeps nothing

Ask Dawn about your goals and your money. Our UK server gets your question and a short summary of your totals, never your transactions, and doesn't keep your questions or figures. If it can't be reached, Apple Intelligence answers on your device.

No ads, no data sales

Freeday has no advertising and we do not sell or rent your data.

Delete whenever you like

Delete your data from within the app or by removing the app, and revoke Wallet access in iOS Settings at any time.
