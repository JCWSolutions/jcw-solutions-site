2. What Freeday stores, and where

What you add, import or connect: your budget, accounts and balances, debts, payslips, goals, life plans and settings, and the transactions you import or connect.

  • On your devices. This data is protected by Apple's data protection, so it's encrypted while your device is locked.
  • In iCloud. If you're signed in to iCloud, it syncs through your private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac each keep a full copy. This is held by Apple under your Apple Account.
  • On Apple Watch and in widgets. Your iPhone sends your Apple Watch the same headline figures the widgets show, such as totals and bills due soon, never transactions. You can hide amounts in widgets in Settings → Privacy.
  • Notion, if you choose it. Freeday can keep your records in your own Notion workspace instead of on your device. If you choose this, your records are written to Notion directly from your device (section 4).
  • No copy on our servers. We don't receive a copy of your financial records, and we can't see them.

3. Apple Wallet (FinanceKit)

If you connect Apple Wallet, iOS asks your permission and you choose which accounts Freeday can read. Freeday reads their balances and transactions on your iPhone to show your spending, bills, budget, net worth and progress. Freeday can't move money. This data:

  • is processed on your iPhone;
  • is stored as described in section 2: on your devices and in your private iCloud or, if you've chosen to keep your records in your own Notion workspace, in Notion;
  • is never sent to us or anyone else as transactions, balances or account details;
  • is never used for advertising, marketing or credit decisions, and is never sold.

One choice is yours. In Settings → Bank Connections, Let Dawn see Wallet spending is off until you turn it on. When it's on, the spending totals Dawn reads when you ask it something, such as this month's spending by category, can include spending from Apple Wallet. They're used to answer you and aren't kept. When it's off, Dawn answers without your spending figures. Either way, Dawn never receives Wallet transactions, shop names or account details.

You can change or remove Freeday's access at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

4. Connections you choose

Freeday can connect to services you choose. When you do, Freeday talks to that service directly from your device. We don't sit in the middle and we don't receive a copy. These are:

  • Notion, if you choose to keep your records in your own Notion workspace;
  • Trading 212, to show your investments;
  • a Google Sheet you link;
  • statement files you import, such as your bank's CSV export;
  • your own Freeday server, if you run one.

Each service's own privacy policy applies to what you send it. Keys you enter (Notion, Trading 212) are kept in iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end, so your other devices signed in to your Apple Account have them too. We can't see them.

Price history. To chart your investments, Freeday downloads public daily prices from Yahoo Finance. The request contains only the ticker symbol, never your holdings, amounts or anything about you. Prices are kept on your device.

5. Dawn, the AI coach

Dawn answers questions about your goals and your money in plain English, and can change your records when you ask and confirm. Dawn is optional.

  • Dawn answers on our server first. Dawn is a language model we run on our own server in the United Kingdom. When you ask it something, it receives only:
    • your question and your last few messages in the conversation;
    • a short summary of figures Freeday has already worked out, such as your net worth, savings rate, FI number and date, debt total, this month's spending against your budget, the plans you've added in Life planner (name, year and cost) and Freeday's current suggestions for you;
    • a text summary of anything you attach to a question (see below).
    It never receives your transactions, merchant names or account details. Its totals include Apple Wallet spending only if you've switched that on (section 3).
  • Apple's on-device model is the fallback. If our server can't be reached, or you've used today's questions, a device with Apple Intelligence answers on the device instead, and nothing leaves it.
  • Your own server: if you choose to use one, the same information goes only to that server.

Requests are tied only to a random key for your installation, not to you, your name or your Apple Account. We don't store your questions, answers or figures. They aren't used to train any AI model, and no other company's AI receives them. Each installation can ask 30 questions a day.

Dawn's server checks the figures in each answer before you see it. If it removes a sentence, it keeps that sentence for quality checks with every digit masked, and nothing about who asked.

Attachments and voice

You can add a photo, screenshot, PDF or bank CSV to a question. Freeday turns it into a short text summary on your device before anything is sent:

  • Photos and PDFs: the text Freeday reads from them. If you attach a statement or receipt, that text can include the amounts and descriptions on it.
  • Bank CSV files: totals by month and category only, never the individual rows.

You see the summary before sending and can edit or remove it. The file itself never leaves your device, and the same applies to photos you take with the camera for Dawn.

If you ask Dawn by speaking, Apple's speech recognition turns your voice into text. Where your device supports it, this happens on the device; otherwise Apple processes the audio under its own privacy policy. We never receive your voice.

Changes Dawn makes

Every change Dawn makes to your records at your request is listed in Settings → Data → Changes made with Dawn. That list is kept on your devices and in your iCloud, not by us.

6. Sharing with your household

If you invite a partner, Freeday shares a small record between your two iCloud accounts using Apple's iCloud sharing. It holds only the totals each of you switches on, such as balances or monthly saving, never transactions, in iCloud's encrypted fields. It doesn't go through any Freeday server, and we can't see it. Either of you can stop sharing at any time in More → Household.

7. Other features that use your data

  • Face ID and Touch ID. You can lock Freeday, or just Wealth, with Face ID or Touch ID. Apple handles this on your device; Freeday never sees your face or fingerprint data.
  • Reminders. Reminders for bills, renewals, debt payments and a monthly check-in are scheduled on your device. They show names, never amounts, and aren't sent through us.
  • Your profile. The name and email you can add on the Account screen, and your Freeday ID, are kept on your devices and in your iCloud so your goals and settings follow you. They aren't sent to us. You don't create an account with us.
  • Feedback. More → Send feedback opens an email to us in your Mail app. We only receive what you choose to send.

8. Purchases

If Freeday offers paid features, such as a Freeday Pro subscription, they are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple Account details. Freeday checks what you've bought on your device, using Apple's StoreKit.

9. What we don't do

  • We have no accounts. You don't sign up to use Freeday.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising tools, and Freeday contains no third-party tracking code. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data.
  • We don't use information from our consultancy clients in Freeday, or Freeday data in our consultancy work.

11. Who else is involved, and where

  • Dawn's server is ours, in the United Kingdom.
  • Cloudflare carries questions securely between your device and Dawn's server, as our service provider. Traffic is encrypted, and Cloudflare may handle it in data centres outside the UK under its data processing terms, with appropriate safeguards.
  • Apple provides iCloud, iCloud Keychain, Apple Wallet, speech recognition and App Store purchases under its own terms.
  • Services you connect (section 4) process data under their own terms and in their own locations.

12. How long we keep information

  • Your financial records: we don't hold them. They stay on your devices, in your iCloud and in services you connect until you delete them.
  • Questions to Dawn, figure summaries and attachment summaries: not stored. They are used to produce the answer and then discarded.
  • Your installation key (stored only as a hash) and the day's question count: deleted after 90 days without use.
  • Sentences removed from Dawn's answers (digits masked, nothing about who asked): only the most recent 300 are kept, and older ones are deleted as new ones arrive.
  • Support and feedback emails: kept for up to 12 months.

13. Security

Freeday relies on Apple's security, including data protection on your devices, iCloud Keychain for keys you enter, Face ID or Touch ID if you turn on the lock, and Apple's encrypted iCloud sync. Dawn's server only accepts encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your data occurs, we will act on it and tell you and the Information Commissioner's Office where the law requires.

15. Deleting your data

In Freeday, Settings → Data → Delete all my data removes everything Freeday keeps on your device and in your iCloud, and any keys you've added. It doesn't change anything at your bank, in Apple Wallet or in Notion. Deleting the app also removes the data on that device. You can remove Apple Wallet access in your iPhone's Settings. Your hashed installation key on Dawn's server can't be linked to you, and is deleted after 90 days without use.
